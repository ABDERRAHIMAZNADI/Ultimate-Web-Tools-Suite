How It Works

This tool adds random suffixes to your email addresses using the plus (+) symbol. For example: shakuntalornima@gmail.com becomes shakuntalornima+54adhth6@gmail.com.

Emails sent to the randomized addresses will still be delivered to your original inbox. This is supported by most major email providers including Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo.
